# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: [26/07/2026]_

PenHaven ("the app", "we", "our") is built on a simple principle: **your
writing belongs to you.** This policy explains, in plain language, exactly
what data the app touches and why.

## What stays on your device

By default, PenHaven is fully offline. The following never leave your
device unless you explicitly enable cloud sync or publish something:

- Your Stories and Entries (text, formatting, images)
- Your To-Dos and Time Capsules
- Your reading preferences, theme, and app settings
- Your PIN (stored only as a salted, one-way cryptographic hash — never in
  plaintext or any reversible form)

## What's sent to our servers (only if you sign in)

If you choose to create an account and enable Sanctuary/cloud features:

- **Account info**: your email address, used solely for authentication.
- **Published entries**: only entries you explicitly choose to publish, and
  only the content of those entries (title, body, optional header image).
  You can publish anonymously — in that case, your name is never attached.
- **Profile info**: display name, bio, and profile/banner photo, if you set
  them.
- **Interactions**: claps, comments, and "Write Backs" you make on public
  content, so counts and threads can be shown to other readers.
- **Anonymized view counts**: used only to show you how many people have
  read your own published work.

Entries you never publish are **never** uploaded, synced, or visible to
anyone but you, even if you're signed in.

## What we don't do

- We don't run ads, anywhere, ever.
- We don't sell or share your data with third parties.
- We don't use your writing to train any model.
- We don't track you across other apps or websites.
- We don't require an account to use the app.

## Third-party services

- **Supabase** — hosts authentication, the optional cloud database, and
  image storage for published content and avatars.
- **Open-Meteo** — a free, keyless weather API used only to determine the
  visual "atmosphere" (e.g. rainy vs. clear) for your device's approximate
  location. No account or personal identifier is sent.
- **OpenStreetMap Nominatim** — used only to resolve your coordinates to a
  city name for weather display, if enabled.

## Your controls

- **Turn off Sanctuary** any time in Settings — this immediately hides all
  of your published content from the community feed without deleting it.
  Turning it back on restores everything exactly as it was.
- **Delete an entry** at any time — deleted entries move to a local Bin and
  can be permanently erased.
- **Delete your account** by contacting us (see below); we will remove your
  published content, profile, and auth record.
- **Export everything** at any time via Settings → Backup & Export, as a
  portable JSON file you control.

## Children's privacy

PenHaven is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly
collect data from them.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a material way, we'll update the "last updated"
date above and, where feasible, notify users in-app.
