# Flow — Vision Document

## The Idea in One Sentence

A writing app that treats your private words as complete and valuable on their
own — and makes sharing feel like a quiet, intentional act, not a performance.

---

## Why Flow Exists

Most writing apps fail writers in the same two ways:

**They make everything feel like a task.** Streaks, reminders, word-count goals,
progress bars. Writing becomes something you fall behind on rather than something
you return to.

**They make privacy feel like incompleteness.** Drafts, publish buttons, share
prompts. An unpublished entry is treated as something unfinished rather than
something personal and whole.

Flow was built to fix both of these. Your writing is not a product to be
shipped. It is not a commitment you are behind on. It is something that happens
when you sit down to write — and stops when you stop — and is complete in either
case.

---

## The Mercy Philosophy

Flow never tells you that you failed. The "mercy rule" means that incomplete
tasks and old entries quietly fade away rather than turning red or nagging. The
app does not remember what you did not do. It only holds what you gave it.

This is not indifference. It is respect. The app trusts you to know your own
life.

---

## What the Sanctuary Is For

The Sanctuary exists for a feeling most people have had but no app has quite
captured: the feeling that something you wrote in private might matter to someone
else, if only they could read it.

Not your followers. Not your audience. Just someone.

The Sanctuary gives writers a place to offer that — anonymously if they want,
under their name if they prefer — and to find writing that resonates from people
they would never otherwise encounter.

It is not designed for growth or reach. It is designed for connection.

---

## Design Philosophy

**Atmosphere over decoration** — The environment adapts to the world. Light,
time, and weather affect how the app looks and feels, making each session
different in a way that mirrors real life.

**Gesture over buttons** — Primary navigation is swipe-based. There are almost
no back buttons. The app is learned by feel, not by reading labels.

**Mercy over judgment** — Nothing turns red. Nothing expires dramatically.
Things fade. The app is gentle with impermanence.

**Editorial over utility** — The app should feel like a beautifully designed
book, not a tool. Typography matters. White space matters. Every pixel is
deliberate.

---

## Long-Term Direction

1. **The writing experience deepens** — more block types, better reading mode,
   better formatting exports. The editor becomes something writers are proud to
   use.

2. **The Sanctuary matures** — better discovery, curated featured content,
   optional collections/series. Still secondary. Still calm. Never algorithmic.

3. **Presence over push** — Flow will never send engagement notifications. It
   will never remind you to write unless you ask it to. The relationship between
   the writer and the app should feel chosen, not engineered.

4. **The app earns its keep** — A fair, one-time or annual pricing model. No
   ads. No data sales. The writer is the customer, not the product.

---

## What Success Looks Like

A writer opens Flow at 11PM after a hard day and writes for an hour. They do not
publish. They do not share. They close the app. They feel better.

That is a successful product.

Everything else — the community, the atmosphere, the themes, the exports — is
there to serve moments like that one.
