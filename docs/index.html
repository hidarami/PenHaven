Features

Built to feel like a place, not a tool.

Every part of PenHaven exists to protect the feeling of sitting down to write without pressure.

✍️

Editorial writing space

A block-based editor with rich formatting, inline images, embeds, and a reading font you can make your own.

🌗

Living atmosphere

Backgrounds and lighting shift with real time-of-day and weather — golden 3PM light, midnight ink, rainy mornings.

🕊️

The Sanctuary

Share entries publicly or anonymously, and read reflections from other writers — entirely optional, never algorithmic.

🕰️

The Mercy Rule

Nothing turns red. Unfinished to-dos quietly fade instead of nagging — the app trusts you to know your own life.

🔒

Private by default

Fully offline-first with an optional PIN + biometric lock. Cloud sync only if you choose to sign in.

📤

Export anywhere

Turn any entry into a PDF, plain text, or a beautifully designed shareable card.
