# PenHaven — Product Document

## What Is PenHaven?

PenHaven is a private writing app — a journaling and long-form writing space built
for people who want to write for themselves first, and share selectively second.

It is not a social network. It is not a productivity tool. It is not a notes
app. PenHaven is a writing sanctuary: a calm, intimate space where your words live
without judgment, pressure, or expectation.

---

## Core Product Pillars

### 1. The Writing Space (Primary)
The main screen is a personal journal. Users create **Stories** (collections)
and write **Entries** within them. The writing experience is full-screen,
distraction-free, and editorially rich — with a block-based WYSIWYG editor,
inline images, multiple content types, and a reading font the user can customize.

Every entry is its own complete thing. There are no drafts. There is no
"publish-ready" vs "unfinished." Your unpublished writing is not a draft waiting
to go somewhere — it is already something, whole and real, just private.

### 2. Atmosphere System
The app's visual environment breathes with the world around the user. The
background, overlays, and lighting shift with the time of day, day of week, and
live weather. A golden 3PM on a clear afternoon looks different from a rainy
midnight. Writing at different times literally feels different.

This is not a theme selector. It is the app being aware of the world.

### 3. The Sanctuary (Secondary — Optional)
The Sanctuary is PenHaven's community panel, placed deliberately in the third
position — to the right of the home screen, not the center. It is not the app.
It is an option within the app.

Users can choose to share selected entries publicly or anonymously. They can
read what others have shared. They can appreciate entries and leave responses.
They can never be forced to participate. The app functions perfectly with the
Sanctuary completely ignored.

The Sanctuary exists because sometimes we want our most personal writing to find
an audience — not for virality, but for resonance. For the feeling that someone
else has been in the same 3AM. The sharing experience is therefore calm and
editorial, not algorithmic or gamified.

## What Makes PenHaven Different

| Typical Journal App | PenHaven |
|---|---|
| Streaks and reminders | No streaks. No guilt. No reminders unless asked. |
| Minimalist but sterile | Editorial richness — atmosphere, typography, art |
| Social-first or social-never | Social-optional, private-first |
| Published = validated | Written = valid, whether shared or not |
| Pressure to be consistent | Mercy rule: old tasks and entries quietly fade |

---

## Target User

PenHaven is built for people who write because they *need* to, not because they
have to. People who have journals but feel let down by journal apps. People who
are neurodivergent, emotional, creative, or simply thoughtful. People who want
their writing to live somewhere that feels like it was built with care.

They are not looking for a productivity boost. They are looking for a place.

---

## What PenHaven Is Not

- Not a Twitter/Instagram competitor
- Not a Notion or Obsidian replacement
- Not an AI writing assistant
- Not a content publishing platform
- Not a streak or habit tracker
- Not a read-it-later app
